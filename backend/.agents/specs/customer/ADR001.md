# Store Only Name in Customer BC and Retrieve Email from Auth BC

## The Problem
We need to determine how customer contact and profile details are modeled across bounded contexts without duplicating identity data or requiring excessive customer information.

## Options Considered
- **Store only customer name in Customer BC and retrieve email from Auth BC via the repository adapter.** - Selected
- Duplicate email storage in Customer BC and maintain a dedicated contact email separate from Auth BC.

## Rationale
Auth BC already contains and manages the user's email address. Having multiple emails or asking for separate contact emails in V1 adds unnecessary complexity and violates the principle of requesting the least amount of information from the customer.

## Notes
Currently, in the modular monolith, the repo adapter handles retrieving the email from Auth BC. If decomposed into microservices later, this can be handled via gRPC or REST API.
