# Restrict Wishlist Support to a Single Wishlist Per Customer for V1

## The Problem
We need to determine the scope and multiplicity of customer wishlists for the initial product release (V1).

## Options Considered
- **Provide a single wishlist per customer as the default in V1, deferring multiple wishlists and merchant toggle configurations to future releases.**
- Support multiple customizable wishlists per customer with merchant configuration options from day one.
- Do not support wishlists at all in V1.

## Rationale
A single wishlist satisfies core e-commerce needs for V1 while keeping domain complexity low. More granular merchant controls (No Wishlist, Single Wishlist, Multiple Wishlists) and multiple lists can be added incrementally in the future.

## Notes
Future iterations may allow admin configuration for wishlist modes (None, Single, Multiple).
