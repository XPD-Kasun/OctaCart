## Step 1 — High-Level Domain Map

### Actors
- **Seller / Admin** — configures base prices on variants, creates promotions and discount rules via admin dashboard.
- **Shopper** — receives a computed final price at browse time and at checkout.
- **Order BC** — requests a resolved, tax-inclusive price when placing an order.

### Business Flows Touching Pricing

1. **Variant Pricing Setup**: Seller creates/updates a variant and sets a base price and optionally a compare-at price. [Inferred — ProductVariant.price + compareAtPrice in product.go]
2. **Storefront Price Display**: Shopper views a product and system resolves effective display price (base, sale, or promo) to show. [Assumed — standard e-commerce pattern]
3. **Checkout Price Resolution**: Shopper proceeds to checkout and Order asks for the final unit price (post-discount, pre-tax or inclusive?) for each variant and Pricing returns a PricedLine. [Assumed]
4. **Tax Calculation**: At checkout, applicable tax rate is applied to the subtotal and results in a TaxedAmount. [Inferred — Settings to Order notes "tax rules consumed by Order"; currently no Tax BC exists]
5. **Coupon / Promo Redemption**: Shopper applies a coupon code and Pricing validates and applies the discount and returns adjusted total. [Assumed — standard small-seller need]

### External Systems
- **Settings BC** — provides currency code and tax rates (Pricing consumes as OHS/PL). [Inferred — context-map: Settings to Order for tax/currency]

## Step 3 — Boundary Analysis: Should Pricing be its own BC?

### Problem signals with absorbing Pricing into Product BC (status quo)
- Price in Product = current display price (mutable by admin). Price in Order = immutable capture at purchase time. These are different models. [Inferred — polyseme documented in context-map.md]
- Tax calculation needs a destination address — Product BC has no concept of a customer or destination.
- Promotions/coupons are time-bound, stackability-checked, budget-capped rules — a very different rate of change from product catalog data. A promo campaign changes weekly; a product title rarely changes.
- Checkout price resolution requires joining variant base price + active promotions + tax rates + coupon codes — complex orchestration that pollutes ProductSvc.

### Boundary justification heuristics

| Heuristic | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Different rates of change | Promotions change weekly; product catalog changes slowly |
| Different domain experts | Merchandising/marketing owns promotions; catalog team owns products |
| Different consistency needs | Coupon budget cap needs a counter; product price is a simple attribute |
| Polyseme Price | Product: display price. Order: locked purchase price. Pricing: computed effective price |
| Regulatory boundary | Tax rates may be jurisdiction-specific; isolating tax logic limits blast radius |
| Data ownership | Pricing rules, promotions, coupon codes, and tax rules need their own tables |

**Verdict**: Pricing is a distinct Supporting BC.

## Step 5 — Ubiquitous Language

| Term | Definition in THIS context |
|---|---|
| Base Price | The standard selling price of a variant, set by the admin. Source of truth before any rules. |
| Compare-At Price | An optional original/crossed-out price displayed to suggest a markdown; purely informational. |
| Effective Price | The unit price after applying all active promotion rules for a given variant and quantity. |
| Discount | A reduction applied to the effective price — either percentage or fixed amount. |
| Promotion | A time-bound pricing rule targeting products, categories, or the whole cart. |
| Coupon | A single-use or limited-use code that unlocks a discount; validated and redeemed at checkout. |
| Redemption | A recorded application of a coupon by a customer at checkout; updates the coupon used-count. |
| Tax Rate | A percentage or fixed amount applied to a line total based on product category and jurisdiction. |
| Tax Label | The human-readable name for the tax line (e.g., "GST 18%", "VAT"). |
| Taxed Amount | The computed tax for a line or order total, expressed in Money. |
| Price Resolution | The process of computing the final PricedLine for a variant+qty at a given moment. |
| PricedLine | The output of price resolution: {variantId, qty, basePrice, effectiveUnitPrice, discount, taxAmount, lineTotal}. |
| Budget Cap | The maximum total discount value a promotion can grant before it deactivates automatically. |
| Stackable | Whether a promotion can be combined with other promotions or a coupon in the same cart. |

| Term | Pricing context | Product context | Order context |

## Step 7 — Validation

### Scenario 1 — Checkout Price Resolution
Shopper adds 2x variant-A to cart. Order calls Pricing.ResolvePrice(variantId=A, qty=2). Pricing fetches base price from Product via read port, matches active promotions, applies bulk discount, adds tax, returns PricedLine. Order stores immutable snapshot.

- Pricing owns no shared writable tables with Order or Product. OK
- One coarse-grained call from Order to Pricing. OK
- Language internally consistent (PricedLine, Promotion, TaxedAmount). OK
- Promotions can evolve (add tiers, stackability) without touching Order or Product. OK

### Scenario 2 — Coupon Redemption
Shopper enters code "SUMMER20". Order calls Pricing.ApplyCoupon(code, cartLines[]). Pricing validates (not expired, not over budget, not already used by this customer). Returns DiscountedCart. Order incorporates into total.

- Coupon budget counter is owned exclusively by Pricing (no shared table). OK
- Redemption record written only by Pricing. OK
- Order gets a clean result; no coupling to Pricing internal rule engine. OK

### Scenario 3 — Admin Creates a Flash Sale
Admin creates Promotion: "30% off category Electronics, 1-2 Sep 2026, budget cap Rs 50,000". Promotion activates. Pricing evaluates it for every relevant ResolvePrice call. Budget exhausted -> Pricing auto-expires promotion -> publishes PromotionExpired.

- Product BC has no knowledge of this promotion. OK
- Order reads already-resolved price; it does not interpret promotion rules. OK
- Single team can own and change the rule engine without touching other BCs. OK

## Bounded Context: Pricing

- **Purpose**: Computes the effective price that a shopper pays for any variant at checkout, by evaluating base prices, active promotions, coupon codes, and applicable taxes — and exposes this as a stable contract to the Order BC.
- **Classification**: Supporting

---

### Entities

- **Promotion** — A time-bound discount rule.
  - `id` (PromoId), `name`, `description`, `kind` (PercentOff | FixedOff | FreeShipping), `discountValue`, `target` (AllProducts | CategoryIds | VariantIds), `targetIds []string`, `startsAt`, `endsAt`, `budgetCap? Money`, `usedAmount Money`, `stackable bool`, `status` (Draft | Active | Expired | Paused)

- **Coupon** — A code-based discount token.
  - `id` (CouponId), `code string`, `promoId PromoId`, `maxUses int?`, `usedCount int`, `perCustomerLimit int?`, `startsAt`, `endsAt`, `status` (Active | Exhausted | Expired | Revoked)

- **Redemption** — A record of a coupon applied by a customer on an order.
  - `id` (RedemptionId), `couponId`, `customerId`, `orderId`, `discountAmount Money`, `redeemedAt`

- **TaxRule** — A tax configuration entry.
  - `id` (TaxRuleId), `label string`, `rate Percentage`, `appliesTo` (All | CategoryIds []), `jurisdiction string?`

> Note: Variant base prices are NOT owned by Pricing. They are read from Product BC via a read port (VariantPricePort). Pricing only stores rules and derived data.

---

### Value Types

- `PromoId`, `CouponId`, `RedemptionId`, `TaxRuleId` — string (opaque, DB-generated)
- `Money` — int64 minor units (from shared.Money)
- `Percentage` — int basis points (e.g., 1800 = 18%)
- `PromoKind` — enum: `PercentOff | FixedOff | FreeShipping`
- `PromoTarget` — enum: `AllProducts | CategoryIds | VariantIds`
- `PromoStatus` — enum: `Draft | Active | Expired | Paused`
- `CouponStatus` — enum: `Active | Exhausted | Expired | Revoked`
- `PricedLine` — `{variantId, qty int, basePrice Money, effectiveUnitPrice Money, discountAmount Money, taxAmount Money, lineTotal Money}` — read-only output value
- `DiscountedCart` — `{lines []PricedLine, couponCode string, couponDiscount Money, taxTotal Money, grandTotal Money}`

---

### Application Services

- **PromotionSvc** (admin)
  - `CreatePromotion(ctx, input CreatePromotionInput)` -> `(Promotion, error)`
  - `UpdatePromotion(ctx, promoId, UpdatePromotionCmd)` -> `(Promotion, error)` — only while Draft or Paused
  - `ActivatePromotion(ctx, promoId)` -> `error`
  - `PausePromotion(ctx, promoId)` -> `error`
  - `ExpirePromotion(ctx, promoId)` -> `error` — marks Expired; idempotent
  - Errors: `ErrPromoNotFound`, `ErrInvalidPromoTransition`, `ErrPromoOverlap`

- **CouponSvc** (admin)
  - `IssueCoupon(ctx, promoId, code, maxUses, perCustomerLimit, startsAt, endsAt)` -> `(Coupon, error)`
  - `RevokeCoupon(ctx, couponId)` -> `error`
  - Errors: `ErrCouponNotFound`, `ErrDuplicateCouponCode`, `ErrPromoNotFound`

- **PriceResolutionSvc** (called by Order at checkout)
  - `ResolvePrice(ctx, variantId, qty int, customerId?)` -> `(PricedLine, error)` — fetches base price, matches active promos, computes effective unit price + tax
  - `ResolveCart(ctx, lines []CartLine, destinationAddress?, customerId?)` -> `(DiscountedCart, error)` — batch resolution for all cart lines
  - `ApplyCoupon(ctx, code, lines []CartLine, customerId)` -> `(DiscountedCart, error)` — validates coupon, applies discount, records pending redemption
  - `ConfirmRedemption(ctx, couponCode, orderId)` -> `error` — called by Order after successful payment; commits redemption and increments usedCount
  - `CancelRedemption(ctx, couponCode, orderId)` -> `error` — called by Order on payment failure; rolls back pending redemption
  - Errors: `ErrVariantNotFound`, `ErrCouponInvalid`, `ErrCouponExpired`, `ErrCouponExhausted`, `ErrCouponAlreadyUsedByCustomer`, `ErrNoActivePromotion`

- **TaxSvc** (admin configuration + internal use)
  - `CreateTaxRule(ctx, input)` -> `(TaxRule, error)`
  - `UpdateTaxRule(ctx, ruleId, input)` -> `(TaxRule, error)`
  - `DeleteTaxRule(ctx, ruleId)` -> `error`
  - `CalculateTax(ctx, lineItems []TaxLine)` -> `(taxAmount Money, breakdown []TaxBreakdown, error)` — invoked internally during price resolution
  - Errors: `ErrTaxRuleNotFound`

- **PricingQuerySvc** (reads — admin dashboard)
  - `GetPromotion(ctx, promoId)` -> `(Promotion, error)`
  - `ListPromotions(ctx, filter PromoFilter, page shared.Pagination)` -> `([]Promotion, error)`
  - `GetCoupon(ctx, couponId)` -> `(Coupon, error)`
  - `ListCoupons(ctx, promoId)` -> `([]Coupon, error)`
  - `ListRedemptions(ctx, couponId, page shared.Pagination)` -> `([]Redemption, error)`
  - `ListTaxRules(ctx)` -> `([]TaxRule, error)`

---

### Domain Services

- **PromotionMatcher** — Given a set of cart lines and the current time, returns all active promotions that apply, sorted by priority (most specific target wins; stackable ones accumulate).
- **DiscountCalculator** — Applies matched promotions and a coupon to compute effectiveUnitPrice per line and overall discountAmount. Enforces stackability rules and budget cap checks.
- **TaxCalculator** — Given a list of {categoryId, amount} pairs, looks up matching TaxRules and computes taxAmount per line. Returns a TaxBreakdown slice.

### Capabilities

- Define and manage time-bound promotion rules (percent-off, fixed-off, free shipping)
- Issue, validate, and redeem coupon codes with usage limits
- Resolve effective unit price for any variant at any point in time
- Calculate applicable tax for cart line items
- Provide a stable price resolution contract for the Order BC at checkout
- Expose promotion/coupon management APIs for the admin dashboard

### Ambiguous Questions Needed From Architect

1. **[Needs Human] Promotion overlap policy**: Can two active promotions apply to the same variant simultaneously? If yes, what is the stacking strategy — best-wins, sum, or explicit stackable flag per promotion?
2. **[Needs Human] Tax inclusion model**: Are prices displayed and stored tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive (tax added at checkout)? This fundamentally changes how PricedLine.lineTotal is computed.
3. **[Needs Human] Tax jurisdiction**: Is tax a single flat rate for all products (e.g., 18% GST on everything) or category/product-specific? Does the destination address affect the rate?
4. **[Needs Human] VariantPriceChanged migration**: Currently VariantPriceChanged is published by Product BC and consumed by Order BC. If Pricing BC owns price resolution, should Order stop consuming this event and rely solely on ResolvePrice calls?
5. **[Needs Human] compareAtPrice ownership**: compareAtPrice is currently on ProductVariant in Product BC. Should it move to Pricing (it is a display-price concept) or stay in Product (it is part of variant data)?
6. **[Assumed] Coupon references one promotion**: Each coupon references exactly one promotion. A promotion can have many coupons (e.g., unique codes per influencer campaign).
7. **[Assumed] Single currency**: Matches Product BC and Settings decisions; Pricing operates in a single currency with no FX conversion.
